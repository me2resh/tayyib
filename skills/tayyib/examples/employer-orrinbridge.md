# Orrinbridge Analytics, Defence Programmes role: Tayyib verdict

Screened on 10 February 2026. Standard: AAOIFI core-business test, then the three ratios; two independent sources must agree. Sample verdict: the company and the figures are illustrative and the links point at example.org; the method and the layout are as shipped.
Verdict: ❌ FAIL

## 1. The verdict in one paragraph

Orrinbridge is a data-analytics consultancy whose group revenue is 71% commercial and public-sector work and 29% defence, so the group passes the core-business test on the 5% rule only if defence is read as incidental, which at 29% it is not 🟡, because the standard's limit is a tenth of that. The role, however, sits in the Defence Programmes unit and the job description names weapons-systems analytics as the work ✅ [^1] [^2]. A role in an excluded unit fails regardless of the group, so the verdict is FAIL ✅ [^3]. A role in the commercial analytics unit would be screened separately and might pass.

## 2. Core business

Group: three units in the FY2025 report, commercial analytics 48% of revenue, public sector 23%, defence programmes 29% ✅ [^4]. Defence at 29% is well above the 5% incidental limit, so the group as a whole is at best BORDERLINE 🟡, because some scholars treat consulting services to defence differently from manufacturing weapons, and the standard's list names defence as the primary business or a unit that serves it. Unit and role: the Defence Programmes unit serves ministries of defence and prime contractors; the job description lists "targeting and sensor-fusion analytics for armoured platforms" ✅ [^1] [^2]. Fail for the role.

## 3. Ratios

Not applicable: the ratios screen an investment; this is employment. If part of the pay is in Orrinbridge shares, screen the shares as a stock before accepting them; the group's debt ratio is 12% and interest income 0.9% ✅ [^4], but the core-business share of defence would still make the shares BORDERLINE at best 🟡 for the reason in section 2.

## 5. Reasoning

The question is the unit and the work, not the group, and the unit and the work are defence by the company's own description ✅ [^1] [^2] [^4]. Salary, title and remote working do not enter the verdict. What would change it: a written offer for a role in the commercial analytics unit with no defence client work, which would be screened as a new case; the group's 29% defence share would still be noted there 🟡, because a mixed group's non-compliant unit can draw on staff from other units.

## 6. Purification

Not applicable to employment. Income already earned from an excluded unit is a matter for the person's own scholar; the verdict does not rule on it.
