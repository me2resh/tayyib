# Quillmark Industries: Tayyib verdict

Screened on 10 February 2026. Standard: AAOIFI core-business test, then the three ratios; two independent sources must agree. Sample verdict: the company and the figures are illustrative and the links point at example.org; the method and the layout are as shipped.
Verdict: ✅ CLEAN

## 1. The verdict in one paragraph

Quillmark makes paper and moulded-fibre packaging for food and consumer goods, with no excluded line of business ✅ [^1]. Interest income is 0.6% of revenue, interest-bearing debt is 18% of market capitalisation and cash plus interest-bearing securities are 9%, all inside the thresholds ✅ [^2] [^3]. Both primary screeners mark it compliant on pages read today, and the tiebreaker agrees ✅ [^4] [^5] [^6]. The verdict changes if debt rises above a third of market capitalisation, which a 45% fall in the share price alone would cause 🟡, because the debt ratio is the nearest to its threshold.

## 2. Core business

Three segments in the FY2025 annual report: consumer packaging 61% of revenue, food-service packaging 31%, recycled fibre 8% ✅ [^1]. No alcohol, tobacco, gambling, finance, defence or entertainment line; the largest customers named are grocers and food brands ✅ [^1] [^7]. Non-compliant income is the finance income line, 0.6% of revenue, below the 5% limit ✅ [^2]. Pass.
