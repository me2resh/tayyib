# Sabrwell Global Equity Index Fund: Tayyib verdict

Screened on 10 February 2026. Standard: AAOIFI core-business test, then the three ratios; two independent sources must agree. Sample verdict: the fund and the figures are illustrative and the links point at example.org; the method and the layout are as shipped.
Verdict: 🟡 BORDERLINE

## 1. The verdict in one paragraph

The fund tracks a named Shariah-screened global equity index with a published methodology and an index-provider Shariah board ✅ [^1] [^2]. Its holdings pass on the index's screen, and the fund holds no bonds or money-market instruments for yield ✅ [^3]. It is BORDERLINE rather than CLEAN for two reasons: the two primary screeners split, one compliant and one "not screened", and the fund publishes no purification policy or figure, so a holder cannot purify without doing the arithmetic across 1,100 holdings 🟡, because the standard requires purification and a fund that does not help with it has not finished its job. A published purification ratio would make it CLEAN.

## 2. Core business

The fund's objective is to track a Shariah-screened index of large and mid-cap companies in developed markets ✅ [^1]. The index methodology excludes the AAOIFI core-business list and applies the three ratios on a 36-month average market capitalisation ✅ [^2]. The top ten holdings are technology, healthcare and consumer companies, none excluded ✅ [^3]. Pass.

The fund itself holds 0.4% in cash for liquidity, not for yield ✅ [^3]. No bonds, no derivatives beyond index futures for cash management, which the methodology permits and the standard tolerates at this size 🟡, because the factsheet states the purpose but not the counterparty.

## 5. Reasoning

The index and its board are the right kind of evidence for a fund, and nothing in the holdings fails ✅ [^1] [^2] [^3]. But the standard asks for two agreeing sources and for purification, and this fund has one source and no purification policy ✅ [^4] [^5] [^7]. BORDERLINE is the honest verdict: the fund is almost certainly compliant in its holdings, and a holder is left to purify unaided. What resolves it: the fund publishing a purification ratio, or Screener B adding it to coverage with a compliant verdict.

## 6. Purification

Not published by the fund ✅ [^7]. The index provider publishes a dividend purification ratio for the index of 1.1% of distributions for 2025 ✅ [^8]. Until the fund publishes its own, apply the index figure: purification owed = 1.1% × distributions received (or, for accumulation units, 1.1% × the income reinvested as shown on the annual statement). Show the arithmetic on each statement.

## 7. Re-screen

On the earlier of the fund publishing a purification policy and 10 August 2026, six months from today; also if the fund changes index or the index provider changes its methodology ✅ [^2].
