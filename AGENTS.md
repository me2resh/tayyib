# Agent instructions for this repository

This repository is a reusable skill called **Tayyib**: a hard halal gate for any financial product or employer, to the AAOIFI standard.

When a task asks whether something is halal or Shariah compliant, asks to recommend, buy, hold or rank any stock, fund, savings or cash product, ISA or pension holding, or asks whether to take a job or a client, read `skills/tayyib/SKILL.md` first and follow it: the standard in `skills/tayyib/references/standard.md`, the sources and procedure in `skills/tayyib/references/sources.md`, the fixed verdict layout in `skills/tayyib/references/outputs.md`, and the checker `skills/tayyib/scripts/check_verdict.py`. Sample verdicts are in `skills/tayyib/examples/`.

Non-negotiable: core business first; two independent sources must agree for CLEAN; a split or unverified source makes it BORDERLINE at best; the verdict is never softened for pay, yield or convenience; every verdict carries a re-screen date.
